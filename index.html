The complete job.

It is in the name. Joe's takes the tree down, deals with what is left in the ground, and hauls the mess away.

  1. 01

    Tree removal

    Dead, damaged or unwanted trees taken down in controlled sections, close to houses, wires and fences.

  2. 02

    Trimming

    Limbs off the roof and away from the lines. Thinned crowns that let light in and wind through.

  3. 03

    Stump grinding

    Stumps ground down below the surface so you can mow, plant or lay sod over the spot.

  4. 04

    Storm cleanup

    Fallen trees and broken limbs cleared off driveways, roofs and yards after the wind dies down.

After a storm

Tree down? Here is the order of things.

Most storm calls start the same way. Knowing the steps makes a bad morning a little easier.

  1. Stay clear

    Keep people, pets and cars away from the tree, and from any wire it touched.

  2. Call the utility

    If a line is down or tangled, the power company comes first. Never move it yourself.

  3. Call Joe's

    Describe what fell and where. Photos from a safe distance help.

  4. Cleared and hauled

    The tree is cut down to size and taken away, stump included if you want it gone.

If a tree is on a power line, treat the line as live. Call your utility or 911 before anyone else.
